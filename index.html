2026

09 Aug on situated cognition in child–AI dialogue
A fragment from fieldwork — kids narrate their reasoning out loud to the agent far more than to a silent worksheet. Not sure yet if this is a novelty effect or something structural about turn-taking.
06 Aug why self-report scales feel wrong for adolescents
Likert scales assume a stable internal reference point. Teens' self-concept shifts week to week — maybe the instrument should be the thing that adapts, not the question.
